Events

Morning

🌸 Guest Registration (8:00 AM – 9:00 AM)

Guests are warmly welcomed and guided to the registration area. A photo wall and guestbook are available for capturing early moments and heartfelt messages. Light refreshments are served to set a cheerful tone before the main program begins. This hour allows everyone to settle in and share anticipation for the day’s events.

🌺 Entourage March (9:00 AM)

The entourage gracefully enters, marking the beginning of a heartfelt celebration. The couple shares personal vows in an intimate moment before close family and friends. Guests are invited to give short words of encouragement or favorite Bible verses. Light merienda with native delicacies is served, symbolizing sweetness and abundance in the couple’s journey ahead.

Ceremony Proper

🌅 Wedding (9:10 AM - 11:00 AM)

The main ceremony unfolds with sincerity and joy as the couple is united in love. A serene setting with tropical blooms and soft pastel drapery enhances the sacred atmosphere. Family and friends witness the meaningful exchange of vows, rings, and the joining of hands. The ceremony concludes with the official declaration of their union as husband and wife.

🎉 Program Proper (12:00 Noon - 2:00 PM)

The celebration continues with a festive program filled with love and laughter. Guests indulge in a traditional Filipino feast while enjoying cultural performances such as tinikling and pandanggo sa ilaw. A romantic first dance beneath a canopy of fairy lights highlights the joy of the couple’s union. The afternoon closes with music, warmth, and memories that everyone will cherish.

Dress Code

Kindly join us in celebrating our special day in elegant attire. We’d love for everyone to dress in style that complements the joy and romance of our wedding.
